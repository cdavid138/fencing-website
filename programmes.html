Programmes

Ways to fence with us

Whether you have never held a sword or you are chasing ranking points, there is a session for you. Everyone starts on a Tuesday.

Start here

Beginners’ course

A structured six-week introduction: on-guard position, footwork, point control, right of way and your first supervised bouts. Kit provided. Run several times a year on Tuesday evenings.

Recreational

Club night

Once you have finished the course, Tuesday becomes your regular fencing night — open fencing, drills and coaching at your own pace, no competition required.

Competitive

Squad training

Thursdays at UU Magee for members working towards competitions: conditioning, tactical work, lessons and hard sparring in épée and sabre.

Youth

Juniors

Young people from around age 9 are welcome at the Tuesday session with a parent or guardian nearby. Sessions are supervised and safeguarding policies apply.

The two weapons

Épée and sabre

Épée

Heavier, with a stiffer blade and a large guard. The whole body is target and there is no right of way — the first to land, or both within a fraction of a second, scores. A patient, distance-based game.

Sabre

A cutting and thrusting weapon derived from the cavalry sword. Target is everything from the waist up, hits land with the edge as well as the point, and bouts are fast and aggressive.

Practical detail

What to bring

  • Comfortable sports clothing — tracksuit bottoms or leggings rather than shorts, and a long-sleeved top.
  • Clean indoor trainers with good grip.
  • A water bottle.
  • For under-18s, a parent or guardian to complete a consent form.

Jackets, masks, gloves and weapons are lent to you while you are learning. Once you decide to stay on, we can advise on buying your own starter set.
