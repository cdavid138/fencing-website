About

A club built by its members

The Fencing Club is a small, volunteer-run club serving Derry~Londonderry and the wider North West. We grew out of fencing at Ulster University’s Magee campus and now run an open community session in Eglinton alongside squad training for competitors.

What we’re about

We want good fencing to be something anyone in the area can take up, not just students or people who started as children. That means a proper beginners’ pathway, loan equipment so cost is not a barrier to trying, and coaches who are happy to teach the fundamentals well.

At the same time we take the sport seriously. Members who catch the competitive bug are supported to enter events and travel with club-mates to compete.

Coaching

Sessions are led by experienced club fencers and British Fencing qualified coaches, supported by senior members who help newcomers find their feet. All coaches hold current safeguarding and first-aid certification.

Competing

Take it as far as you like

Plenty of members are happy fencing socially on a Tuesday and never enter a competition. For those who want to test themselves, the Thursday squad prepares fencers for open events in épée and sabre across Ireland, and we travel to competitions together as a club.

Safeguarding & safety

Fencing is a controlled combat sport with an excellent safety record. All fencing is done in protective clothing rated for the sport, on marked pistes, under supervision. We follow British Fencing’s codes of conduct and safeguarding policy, and juniors are always supervised. Our welfare officer can be reached through the club email.
