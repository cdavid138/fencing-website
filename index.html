Why fencing

A sport of nerve, timing and tactics

Often called “physical chess”, fencing rewards quick thinking as much as quick feet. It is a full-body workout, welcoming at any age, and one of the few Olympic sports you can genuinely start as an adult and still compete.

Start from scratch

Our six-week beginners’ course covers footwork, blade work and your first bouts, with all equipment provided.

Both weapons

Learn épée and sabre and find the one that suits you. Coaches guide you as you progress.

Compete when ready

Our Thursday squad trains for regional and national events under British Fencing rules.

A friendly club

We are a small, volunteer-run club. Members range from schoolchildren to those fencing well into their sixties.
