Join & contact

Come and try it

The easiest way to start is simply to turn up on a Tuesday. Your first session is free and there is no need to commit to anything.

How to join

  1. Email us first at info@thefencing.club so we know to expect you and can confirm the course dates.
  2. Turn up on Tuesday at Eglinton Community Centre for 6:00pm, in comfortable sports clothing and clean trainers.
  3. Borrow our kit — jacket, mask, glove and weapon are provided while you learn.
  4. Decide at your own pace. If you enjoy it, we’ll sort out membership and British Fencing registration.

Common questions

Do I need any experience or equipment?

No. The beginners’ course assumes you have never fenced. We lend you everything you need until you are ready to buy your own.

How much does it cost?

Your first session is free. After that we charge a small fee per session or per term to cover hall hire and equipment, plus an annual British Fencing membership once you decide to stay. Email us for the current rates.

How old do you have to be?

We take juniors from around age 9, with a parent or guardian present, up to adults of any age. Fencing is genuinely a lifelong sport.

Is it safe?

Yes. Fencing has a very low injury rate. All bouts are fought in protective kit rated for the sport, on marked pistes, under supervision, following British Fencing rules.

Which weapon will I learn?

The club fences épée and sabre. Beginners try both during the course — épée for its patient, whole-body game and sabre for its speed — then focus on whichever suits them.
